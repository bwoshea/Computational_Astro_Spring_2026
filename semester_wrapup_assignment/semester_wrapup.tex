The goal of this final pre-class assignment is to have you spend a bit
of time reflecting on what you've learned in this class and how it
might be useful to you in the future.  The idea is for you to
synthesize and summarize your thoughts about the course, but don't
spend too much time on this.  \textbf{Please spend no more than one
hour on this entire assignment!}  To be clear, there are not right or
wrong answers here -- I just want to hear your thoughts about what
you've learned from this class!  This will also inform the discussion
that we have in class the day after the assignment is done.

In the included \texttt{ANSWERS.md} file, please answer the following
questions:

\begin{enumerate}

\item Please take a bit of time to look back through the various 
pre-class and in-class assignments before answering this question!  What 
were the most interesting and/or important things that you've learned from 
this class?

\item When you think about the arc of the entire semester, what 
were the broad themes that emerged from the topics that we've explored?  
In other words, what were your key "big picture" takeaways from this class 
in terms of numerical and statistical methods?  (Note that I'm not looking
for specific topics here - I'm really looking for a synthesis of the 
overarching themes you took away from the course.)

\item How do you think what you've learned in this class will be 
useful to you in the future?  (Other than making you a real challenge for 
seminar speakers during the Q\&A period, that is.)

\item What topics did you learn about in this class that you would 
have liked us to spend more time on, or topics that we didn't cover that you 
wish we had done?

\item What are your plans for the summer, both for work and personally?

\end{enumerate}


In addition to answering those questions in the included markdown
file,
please take a few 
minutes to fill out the online SIRS form (found at 
[https://sirsonline.msu.edu/](https://sirsonline.msu.edu/) ) 
so I can get your feedback on how the class went!  In the free-response area 
of the SIRS form, I usually ask students to answer three questions:

\begin{enumerate}

  \item What aspects of the class helped you learn the materials?
  \item What aspects of class could I improve next time I teach this class?
  \item Do you have any other comments about this class, or suggestions for how I can improve my teaching?

\end{enumerate}

It would be great if you could answer those, or some subset of them, because
the written feedback on my teaching is a lot more useful to me than the numerical
ratings.
